FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear details about contracts, site visits, turnkey milestones, and billing structures.

What construction services does KNS Construction & Real Estate provide?

KNS delivers full-spectrum construction and real estate services: architectural 2D/3D planning, structural engineering analysis, RCC civil construction, turnkey residential construction, commercial construction, interior finishing, exterior elevation work, and plotted villa community development.

What is turnkey construction by KNS?

Turnkey construction is our single-partner execution model managing all 7 sequential stages: 01 Design, 02 Planning, 03 Structure, 04 Civil Work, 05 Electrical + Plumbing, 06 Flooring + Finishing, and 07 Handover with milestone-based billing and quality supervision.

What are KNS construction rates per square foot?

Our verified packages are: Standard Residential (₹1,600 – ₹1,800 / SFT), Premium Residential (₹1,900 – ₹2,200 / SFT), and Commercial Construction (₹2,200 – ₹2,500 / SFT). All rates include quality materials and supervision. Indicative pricing varies by design, location, specifications, and client requirements.

What geographic areas does KNS serve?

Headquartered in Raghavendra Colony, Kondapur, Hyderabad, we actively construct residential and commercial projects across Hyderabad, Kondapur, Gachibowli, Patancheru, Sangareddy, and surrounding growth corridors in Telangana.

What are the details of the G+1 Duplex House project?

Our turnkey G+1 duplex model in Raghavendra Colony, Kondapur is built on a 200 Sq. Yard East-facing plot with approximately 2,800–3,200 SFT total built-up area (~1,400–1,600 SFT ground floor and ~1,400–1,600 SFT first floor).

What are the details of the Premium 3BHK project?

Our premium residential apartment project in Raghavendra Colony, Kondapur is situated on a 600 Sq. Yard site and features 10 exclusive 3BHK units of 1,650 SFT each with East-facing orientation.

What is the KNS Premium Villa Community and what are the plot dimensions?

The KNS Premium Villa Community is an 8.25-acre gated plotted development featuring 100 planned villa plots of 250 Sq. Yards each (standard 30 × 75 ft geometry) with 40 ft wide internal roads in Patancheru / Sangareddy.

What amenities are planned in the KNS Villa Community?

The master plan includes 10 dedicated amenities: Parks & Open Space, Clubhouse, Swimming Pool, Children's Play Area, Grand Entry / Exit Gate, Security Cabin, Visitor Parking, Street Lighting, Underground Drainage & Utilities, and an STP & Water Treatment Plant.

How does KNS structure construction billing and payments?

Payments are divided into stage-wise milestone disbursements tied strictly to physical verification: Foundation casting, Plinth beam, Slab cast, Brickwork, Plastering, Electrical/Plumbing, Flooring, and Final handover. You never pay in advance of certified milestones.
