Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding timelines, contracts, billing structures, and RERA certifications.

How are project payment milestones structured?

Payments are tied strictly to physical on-site completion milestones (e.g., Plinth completion, 1st slab casting, brickwork, plastering, and finishes). You only pay after our quality audit team certifies each phase.

Are all KNS real estate developments RERA certified?

Yes, 100% of our residential communities and commercial projects hold verified RERA registration numbers, DTCP/BDA sanctions, and clear freehold legal titles vetted by top legal counsels.

What structural warranty does KNS provide?

We back every civil structure we construct with an ironclad 10-year structural warranty covering RCC frames, foundations, and load-bearing elements, backed by certified material test certificates.
