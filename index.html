ABOUT KNS

BUILDING DREAMS, BRICK BY BRICK

KNS Construction & Real Estate is a trusted construction, engineering, and property development firm based in Kondapur, Hyderabad. Founded by Kiran Gajbhare, we focus on delivering dependable turnkey construction, architectural planning, 2D/3D design, structural engineering, civil construction, and interior/exterior finishing.

Whether developing independent residences, multi-family apartments, commercial buildings, or master-planned villa plot communities, our execution is guided by engineering discipline, quality materials, and transparent client engagement.

COMPREHENSIVE CAPABILITIES

OUR SERVICES

From conceptual planning to structural execution and interior handover, we provide complete engineering and construction solutions.

01

ARCHITECTURAL PLANNING

Comprehensive layout planning, functional space optimization, municipal compliance, and architectural drawings for residential and commercial structures.

02

2D / 3D DESIGNING

Detailed 2D working floor plans, sections, elevations, and high-fidelity 3D exterior/interior architectural visualizations before groundbreaking.

03

STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING

Load-bearing structural analysis, reinforced cement concrete (RCC) design, foundation detailing, and structural safety calculations.

04

CIVIL CONSTRUCTION

Foundation excavation, footing, columns, beams, slab casting, and quality masonry brickwork constructed with high-grade materials.

05

TURNKEY CONSTRUCTION

End-to-end single-partner execution managing design, raw materials, civil structural works, MEP installations, finishes, and final keys handover.

06

ELECTRICAL WORKS

Concealed conduit piping, fire-resistant modular wiring, distribution boards, earthing protection, and designer architectural lighting fixtures.

07

PLUMBING WORKS

High-pressure CPVC/UPVC water pipelines, sanitary drainage networks, overhead water storage, and branded bathroom plumbing fittings.

08

FLOORING

Installation of premium vitrified tiles, granite, marble, anti-skid bathroom surfaces, and parking pavers with leveled cement base.

09

INTERIOR FINISHING

Wall putty, emulsion painting, designer false ceilings, door frames, kitchen countertops, and fine woodwork finishes.

10

EXTERIOR FINISHING

Weather-shield exterior texture paint, architectural stone cladding, compound wall construction, and aesthetic facade detailing.

11

COMMERCIAL CONSTRUCTION

Turnkey construction for commercial office spaces, retail showrooms, shopping complexes, and institutional facilities.

12

REAL ESTATE

Planned residential villa community layouts, independent houses, apartments, and commercial property developments.
