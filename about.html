OUR FOUNDATION

BUILDING DREAMS, BRICK BY BRICK

KNS Construction & Real Estate is a comprehensive architectural planning, engineering, civil construction, and real-estate firm based in Kondapur, Hyderabad. Under the leadership of Kiran Gajbhare, we have earned our reputation on the strength of honest execution, dependable timelines, and meticulous craftsmanship.

We specialize in complete turnkey construction, handling every facet from initial municipal layout drawings, 2D working drafts, and 3D architectural renders to foundation footing, structural RCC casting, brickwork, electrical and plumbing infrastructure, and premium interior and exterior finishes.

In real estate, we develop planned communities such as the flagship KNS Premium Villa Community at Patancheru Mandal, Sangareddy District, bringing structured infrastructure, wide asphalt avenues, and clear plot layouts to prospective homeowners.
